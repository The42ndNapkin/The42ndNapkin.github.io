About Me

What's up guys! I'm Alexander Daley. I'm a graduate of Champlain College with a Game Programming Major and a CSI/Math dual minor. I started coding using Scratch in middle school and I never looked back from there!
My experience thus far has been a variety of collaborative and solo projects in a school environment, although I've been involved in a couple extracurricular projects with my peers too! I'm looking to get experience in the industry however I can, although I would like to find a steady location where I can develop games for a consistent community in the future! If that interests you feel free to contact me! I'm a pretty transparent person so I like to be open and communicative about my work. I'm not afraid to ask questions and I'm a pretty fast learner. If you're curious as to what I can do, feel free to take a look around!
