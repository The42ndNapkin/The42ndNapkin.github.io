Domain Expansion

Y2K is just around the corner and the internet is just starting to pick up. Your keen business eye has noticed the incoming website domain bubble and you, living out of your mother's basement wanna make a quick buck or 2. Buy website domains the old-fashioned way and let their values rise as much as possible before selling them for a quick profit! Just make sure you sell your websites before the New Year because once Y2K hits, that bubble's gonna burst!

Buy website domains and sell them for a profit! Chase the highest score you can!

This quick and simple score chaser was made for the 2025 Global Game Jam.

My Contributions:

Objective System:

I did the entire objective system, which expands the depth of gameplay allowing dinosaurs to ignore enemies in favor of attacking objectives for additional benefits.

Dinosaur Abilities:

Each dinosaur has their own unique ability which has gone through a wide range of balance changes throughout development. I have assisted in the implementation and iteration of these abilities leading to the current game balance.

Pack Opening:

My implementation of the shop tiers led to me modifying and fixing the rarity drop chances as well as the physical space by the shop.

Marks and Flairs:

I also helped test and fix some flairs and marks on top of the dinosaur abilities.
