Domain Expansion

Y2K is just around the corner and the internet is just starting to pick up. Your keen business eye has noticed the incoming website domain bubble and you, living out of your mother's basement wanna make a quick buck or 2. Buy website domains the old-fashioned way and let their values rise as much as possible before selling them for a quick profit! Just make sure you sell your websites before the New Year because once Y2K hits, that bubble's gonna burst!

Buy website domains and sell them for a profit! Chase the highest score you can!

This quick and simple score chaser was made for the 2025 Global Game Jam.

My Contributions:

Score System/Website System:

I handled the player score which is made up of the amount of money they have, as well as the appreciation of their owned domain names and the inflation from website trends. This meant I also had to implement the purchased website system, and I also helped with the website interface visuals.

Sound Fx/Music Implementation:

I implemented the sound effects and the music into the game.
