Prehistoric Pack

Prehistoric Pack is a dinosaur-themed auto battler. Each turn, rip open card packs of dinosaurs to create a ferocious dinosaur pack. Then your dinosaurs will automatically battle an enemy team. Win rounds to collect bones and open more packs, then fill the Apex Meter to unleash devastating power on your prey!

Open packs and collect dinosaurs!

Pit your dinos against teams of enemies!

My Contributions:

Objective System:

I did the entire objective system, which expands the depth of gameplay allowing dinosaurs to ignore enemies in favor of attacking objectives for additional benefits.

Dinosaur Abilities:

Each dinosaur has their own unique ability which has gone through a wide range of balance changes throughout development. I have assisted in the implementation and iteration of these abilities leading to the current game balance.

Bug Fixes:

Pack Opening:

My implementation of the shop tiers led to me modifying and fixing the rarity drop chances as well as the physical space by the shop.

General Gameplay:

I also helped test and fix some flairs and marks.
